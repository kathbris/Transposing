How to play
  • Drag a tile across the = sign to move it to the other side.
  • Something multiplying on one side goes to the bottom (divides) on the other side.

  • A term that is added becomes subtracted on the other side, and vice versa — drag the whole term.
  • Use √ both sides to remove a square, and x² both sides to remove a square root.
